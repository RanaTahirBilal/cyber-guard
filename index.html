2019 — the problem

Everything was available.
None of it was in order.

Every answer he needed already existed somewhere on the internet. That was the problem. Twenty‑three things to learn, no sequence, and no way to tell which one came first.

Twenty-three, in no order at all.

Which is exactly how it looked to him.

2019 – 2020

A year of this.

Not a montage. Attrition. The same problem, forty times, until the fortieth time it worked and he could not fully explain why.

The year people thought I was wasting turned out to be the most valuable investment of my life.

Why it matters

Most people are not attacked.
They are asked.

  1. 01A message arrives.It looks like something you were already expecting.
  2. 02You click.Nothing appears to go wrong. That is the design.
  3. 03You sign in.The page looks right. It is not the page.
  4. 04Somebody else is in.Your password now belongs to two people.
  5. 05Then, nothing.For weeks. The gap between step four and the day you find out is where the damage happens.

Nothing above requires technical skill to fall for — which is the whole reason he explains it in plain language rather than jargon.

He never forgot what it felt like
